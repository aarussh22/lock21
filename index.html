The Story Behind Lock21

Lock21 is more than a name it represents Peterborough's identity, history, and spirit of progress

Inspired by the iconic Peterborough Lift Lock, Canada's highest hydraulic lift lock, Lock21 symbolizes connection, movement, and community. For over a century, the Lift Lock has brought people together, helping them move forward while standing as a reminder of what innovation and determination can achieve.

Built on that same philosophy, Lock21 is a premium local rewards and loyalty platform created to strengthen the relationship between Peterborough's residents and its local businesses. Every purchase, visit, and reward helps create stronger customer loyalty, encourages repeat business, and supports the growth of the local economy.

Just as the Lift Lock raises boats to new heights, Lock21 is designed to elevate local businesses by increasing customer retention, improving customer engagement, and rewarding shoppers for choosing local. It transforms everyday purchases into meaningful connections between businesses and the community they serve
